# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, safe, and constructive community for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, specific, and kind.
- Critique ideas and code without attacking people.
- Welcome questions and make room for different levels of experience.
- Respect privacy, attribution, licensing, and project boundaries.
- Accept moderation decisions and correct harmful behavior.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, sustained disruption, and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through the contact method listed in the owning GitHub organization. Do not use public issues for sensitive reports.

Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or ban contributors when necessary to protect the community. Reports will be handled as privately and promptly as practical.

## Scope

This policy applies in project repositories, community spaces, and public interactions where someone is representing Polterware.
